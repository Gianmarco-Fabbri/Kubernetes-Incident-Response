\section*{Project origin and attribution}
This project was originally designed and developed by Gianmarco Fabbri during
his M.Sc. studies in Cybersecurity Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, and was
subsequently extended with additional response controls and automated tests.
It builds on existing runtime-security teaching material; the current implementation
and the original material should not be treated as the same contribution.

The author acknowledges Francesco Pizzato, who created the original laboratory
material, and Alex Palesandro, Marco Iorio and Stefano Galantino for their
enhancements to that material. These credits are retained independently of the
technical presentation of this report.
